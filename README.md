Vesper City: an original open-world crime game in the browser, built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

Rook Calloway owes fixer Marlowe Vane five thousand dollars, and Marlowe collects in favours. Explore the sun-soaked city of Vesper City on foot or by car — steal cars, run packages, dodge the Vesper City PD and work off the debt across a 4-mission story chain, or just cause trouble in free roam. The city has a 9×9 road grid with traffic that stops for jams and pedestrians, pedestrians who flee danger, a 5-star wanted system with pursuing police cars and foot cops, melee and firearm combat, a HUD with a live minimap, a day clock, WebAudio sound (shots, sirens, engine hum, mission stingers) and a localStorage save with continue.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
